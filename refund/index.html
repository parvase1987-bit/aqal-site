Refund policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

  • Within 14 days of a payment: your money back in full. No questions asked.
  • After that, on a quarterly or yearly plan: you get back what's left once each month you've started is paid for at the monthly price. Premium stays open to the end of the month you're in.
  • This applies to payments on the web and in Google Play. On iPhone and iPad, Apple decides refunds.

An example

You pay $39.99 for a year on 1 December and cancel on 10 February. December, January and February have started, so 3 months count at the monthly price: 3 × $4.99 = $14.97. You get back $39.99 − $14.97 = $25.02, and Premium stays open until 1 March. The amount is always worked out in the currency you paid, from what you actually paid.

How to ask

  • In the app: Aqal Premium → Cancel subscription. The app shows the refund it works out and sends us the request.
  • Already cancelled in Google Play or on paddle.net? Use Contact Aqal in the app's Settings, or write to aqal.app.academy@gmail.com.
  • We send refunds ourselves within a few days and email you when it's done. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with; your bank may take another 5 to 10 working days to show it.
  • After a refund, Premium ends as above and the free lessons stay open. Your progress stays in your account.

Bought on the web (aqal.academy)

  • Our order process is run by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle is the Merchant of Record for web orders and handles payment and billing questions. You can also reply to your Paddle receipt email or look up your order at paddle.net.
  • You can cancel at any time. Premium then stays open until the end of the period you paid for (or the month you're in, if you take a refund), and you aren't charged again.

Bought in the app

  • Google Play: the rule above applies, and we send the refund. Google also gives refunds itself in the first 48 hours: request a refund from Google.
  • App Store (iPhone, iPad): Apple takes the payment and decides refunds; we can't send money back for App Store payments. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If they say no and you feel that's unfair, write to us and we'll do what we can to help.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights under the law where you live. It doesn't take any of them away.
