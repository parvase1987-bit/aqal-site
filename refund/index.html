Bought on the web and Premium isn't right for you? Ask within 14 days of paying and you get your money back in full. No questions asked.

Bought in the app (Google Play or App Store)

Google and Apple take the payment, so they handle refunds under their own rules:

If they say no and you feel that's unfair, write to us and we'll do what we can to help.
