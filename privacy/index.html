Aqal: Understand the Quran

Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Aqal is run by Mohamed Parvase M, United Arab Emirates. Contact: parvase1987@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can use the first lesson without an account. Everything then stays on your phone.
  • If you create an account, we keep your learning progress so it follows you to any phone.
  • We don't sell your data, and the app has no advertising and no third-party ad or tracking tools.
  • You can delete your account and everything saved with it at any time, from inside the app.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhenWhy
Email address (and your name, if you sign in with Google)When you create an accountTo sign you in and send account emails (confirm your account, reset your password)
Your learning progress: lessons done, XP, streak, daily goal, words and Ayat you saved, settingsSaved on your phone; also online once you have an accountSo your progress is never lost and follows you to a new phone
Lesson statistics: when you start, leave (on which page) or finish a lesson, and which questions you got right the first timeOnly with an accountTo find confusing pages and improve the lessons
Problem reports you choose to send: your message, where in the app you were, the app version and phone modelWhen you tap "Report a problem"To fix mistakes
Free-access requests you choose to send ("Can't afford Premium?")When you send oneTo decide on free access
Premium status: whether you have Premium and until whenIf you subscribe or get free accessTo open the Premium lessons

We never see your card or bank details. Subscriptions are paid through Google Play. RevenueCat, our subscription service, tells us only that a subscription exists and when it ends.

Word pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. The word is spoken on your phone and isn't sent to us.

Who processes your data for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in Seoul, South Korea: stores your account and progress.
  • Google (Google Play billing; Google sign-in if you choose it).
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions (no card details).
  • An email delivery service: sends account emails.

We share data with these services only so they can do their part of the app, never to sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: until you delete your account.
  • Problem reports: kept to improve the app. If you delete your account, they stay with your account removed from them.
  • Data on your phone: until you log out, reset your progress or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your account and everything saved with it, on every phone, and can't be undone. Without the app, see Delete your Aqal account.
  • See or correct your data: email parvase1987@gmail.com.
  • If you subscribe: deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.

Age

Aqal is meant for learners aged 14 and over. Younger learners can use it together with a parent, on the parent's account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app when the change matters.
