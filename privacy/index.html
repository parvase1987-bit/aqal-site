Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Aqal is run by Mohamed Parvase M, United Arab Emirates. Contact: aqal.app.academy@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can use the first lesson without an account. Everything then stays on your phone.
  • If you create an account, we keep your learning progress so it follows you to any phone.
  • We don't sell your data, and the app has no advertising and no third-party ad or tracking tools.
  • Your location, if you use prayer-time reminders, stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • You can delete your account and everything saved with it at any time, from inside the app.

We never see your card or bank details. Subscriptions are paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. RevenueCat, our subscription service, tells us only that a subscription exists and when it ends.

Prayer-time reminders and your location. If you choose reminders after prayers, the app asks your phone for your approximate location once, rounds it to about 10 km, and works out the prayer times on your phone. The location is kept on your phone only: it isn't sent to us or to anyone else, and isn't saved with your account. You can remove it in the app (Settings → Daily reminder → Forget my location) or refuse it and use your own reminder times instead.

Reminders are planned by your phone itself, at the times you choose. They don't go through a server.

Word pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. The word is spoken on your phone and isn't sent to us.

Who processes your data for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in Seoul, South Korea: stores your account and progress.
  • Google: Google Play billing, and Google sign-in if you choose it.
  • Apple: App Store billing, and Sign in with Apple if you choose it (Apple may give us a private relay email address instead of your own, if you ask it to hide your email).
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions (no card details).
  • Resend: sends account emails, and tips and updates if you asked for them.
  • Sentry: receives a crash report when the app breaks (the screen, the error, the app version and phone model; no name, email or location), so we can fix it.

We share data with these services only so they can do their part of the app, never to sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: until you delete your account.
  • Problem reports: kept to improve the app. If you delete your account, they stay with your account removed from them.
  • Waiting-list emails: until Aqal is available and we've told you, or until you ask us to remove yours.
  • Data on your phone: until you log out, reset your progress or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your account and everything saved with it, on every phone, and can't be undone. Without the app, see Delete your Aqal account.
  • See or correct your data: email aqal.app.academy@gmail.com.
  • Tips and updates: switch them off in the app, Settings → Tips and updates by email, or email us.
  • If you subscribe: deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play or, on iPhone and iPad, in your Apple ID settings.

Age

Aqal is meant for learners aged 13 and over. Younger children can learn with a parent, on the parent's account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app when the change matters.
